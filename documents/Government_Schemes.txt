# Master Directory of Agricultural Government Schemes & Subsidies (India)
Document Ref: MOA-SCHEMES-2025-26
Source: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India

## 1. Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
- Purpose: Direct income support to landholding farmer families to supplement financial needs for agricultural inputs and domestic obligations.
- Benefit: Direct benefit transfer (DBT) of ₹6,000 per year, disbursed in three equal installments of ₹2,000 every 4 months directly into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts.
- Eligibility: All landholding farmer families with cultivable land in their names. Excludes institutional landholders, tax-paying professionals, constitutional post holders, and retired pensioners with >₹10,000/month pension.
- Mandatory Requirement: e-KYC via OTP or biometric face authentication on PM-KISAN portal, and Aadhaar-bank account linking.

## 2. Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) - Crop Insurance
- Purpose: Comprehensive risk insurance against non-preventable natural risks (drought, flood, unseasonal rains, pest epidemics, post-harvest losses within 14 days of cut).
- Farmer Premium Rates:
  - Kharif crops (Food & Oilseeds): Maximum 2.0% of Sum Insured.
  - Rabi crops (Food & Oilseeds): Maximum 1.5% of Sum Insured.
  - Annual Commercial & Horticultural crops: Maximum 5.0% of Sum Insured.
  - Balance premium is heavily subsidized by Central and State Governments (50:50).
- Claim Intimation: Must report crop damage within 72 hours via the Crop Insurance App, National Crop Insurance Portal (NCIP), or local bank/KVK.

## 3. Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) - Per Drop More Crop (PDMC)
- Purpose: Enhancing water use efficiency through Micro Irrigation (Drip & Sprinkler).
- Financial Assistance:
  - Small and Marginal Farmers (landholding up to 2 hectares): 55% subsidy of benchmark installation cost.
  - Other Farmers: 45% subsidy of benchmark installation cost.
  - Top-up subsidies: Several state governments provide an additional 15-25% top-up, bringing the effective subsidy up to 70-90% for small farmers.

## 4. Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY) - Organic Farming
- Purpose: Promotion of commercial organic farming through cluster formation and PGS (Participatory Guarantee System) certification.
- Financial Assistance: ₹50,000 per hectare for 3 years, of which ₹31,000/ha is directly provided for organic inputs (bio-fertilizers, vermicompost, botanical extracts, seeds).
- Target: Cluster size of 20 hectares with 50 or more farmers.

## 5. Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme
- Purpose: Timely access to short-term credit for cultivation, post-harvest expenses, produce marketing, and working capital for dairy/fisheries.
- Interest Subvention: Concessional interest rate of 7% per annum for loans up to ₹3,00,000. Prompt repayment incentive provides a further 3% rebate, making effective interest rate only 4% per annum.
- Collateral-free limit: Up to ₹1.60 lakh without collateral security.
