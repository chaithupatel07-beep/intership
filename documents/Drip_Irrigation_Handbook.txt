# Smart Water Management & Precision Drip Irrigation Handbook
Document Ref: WTC-ICAR-IRRIG-2025
Source: Water Technology Centre, ICAR & National Mission on Micro Irrigation

## 1. Principles of Precision Irrigation
Precision irrigation matches water application to crop evapotranspiration (ETc), avoiding deep percolation losses and surface runoff.
- Drip Irrigation Efficiency: 90-95% water use efficiency compared to 35-40% in traditional flood basin irrigation.
- Water Savings: 40% to 60% reduction in water consumption with 20% to 35% higher yields due to minimal root stress.
- Fertigation Advantage: Uniform delivery of water-soluble fertilizers directly to the active root zone, cutting fertilizer losses by 30%.

## 2. Crop-specific Water Requirements and Critical Growth Stages
- Paddy (Rice):
  - Total water requirement: 1100 - 1250 mm.
  - Critical Stages: Panicle initiation to flowering. Water stress at flowering can lead to 40-50% grain sterility.
  - Water-saving technique: Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) using a perforated pani-pipe saves 25% water without yield reduction.
- Maize (Corn):
  - Total water requirement: 500 - 600 mm.
  - Critical Stages: Tasseling, silking, and early grain filling.
- Cotton:
  - Total water requirement: 700 - 900 mm.
  - Critical Stages: Square formation, flowering, and boll development.
- Groundnut:
  - Total water requirement: 450 - 550 mm.
  - Critical Stages: Flowering, pegging, and pod development. Moisture stress at pegging stops peg penetration.
- Tomato & Vegetables:
  - Total water requirement: 400 - 600 mm.
  - Critical Stages: Flowering and fruit set.

## 3. Drip Irrigation System Maintenance Checklist
- Operating Pressure: Maintain lateral line pressure at 1.0 to 1.2 kg/cm2 (bar).
- Filter Cleaning:
  - Sand Media Filter: Backwash daily or when pressure drop exceeds 0.5 kg/cm2.
  - Screen/Disc Filter: Clean flushing valve after every irrigation cycle.
- Acid Treatment for Dripper Clogging: If emitters are clogged with calcium/carbonate deposits (hard water), flush laterals with Hydrochloric acid (HCl) @ 0.6% concentration for 24 hours, then flush with clean water.
- Chlorination: Inject sodium hypochlorite (bleaching powder solution @ 20 ppm) once a month to clear algae and bacterial slime.
